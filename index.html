Privacy Policy for Sitka EMS

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Sitka EMS ("the App") is a code-blue documentation tool for emergency medical services. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

The App collects the following data solely on your device:

  • Voice recordings — The App uses your device's microphone to record optional voice notes attached to resuscitation events. These recordings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted.
  • Code event logs — Resuscitation event data (timings, drugs administered, shocks, rhythms) is stored locally in the App's private storage.

Data Sharing

The App does not share, transmit, sell, or upload any data to external servers. There is no internet connectivity in the App.

Data Export

You may export a PDF summary of a resuscitation event using your device's share sheet. You control where that export is sent.

Data Deletion

All data can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing App data in your device settings.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — Used exclusively to record voice notes. Recording only occurs when you explicitly initiate it within the App.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: SitkaEMS@gmail.com
